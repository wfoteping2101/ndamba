A full-page illustration for a French children's adventure novel about football (readers aged 8 to 12). Painted in a warm, hand-painted animated-film style reminiscent of classic Japanese animation: soft gouache and watercolour textures, gentle cel shading, clean expressive linework, rich detailed backgrounds, luminous natural light, cinematic composition, a feeling of wonder and warmth. The characters are Black Cameroonian children and adults drawn with natural, respectful, realistic proportions and features (not caricatured, not chibi).

A character design sheet: the two Cameroonian brothers standing side by side, full body, front view, on a plain warm cream background with a soft ground shadow. On the left, Kaydoudou, 11 years old; on the right, BriBri, 10 years old. Kaydoudou (the elder brother): slim and tall for his age, dark brown skin, very short black hair with a thin part shaved on the left side, calm thoughtful eyes, a small confident smile; his favourite shirt is a faded forest-green football shirt with a white collar and the number 8. BriBri (the younger brother): shorter, round cheeks, dark brown skin, a small soft afro, a big gap-toothed grin, sparkling mischievous eyes, always up on his toes; his signature is one red sock and one yellow sock. Kaydoudou wears his green number 8 shirt, black shorts, white socks and worn black boots, and holds under his arm an old leather football patched with a square of red-and-yellow wax-print cloth. BriBri wears a sun-yellow T-shirt with one red stripe, green shorts, one red sock and one yellow sock, scuffed trainers, caught mid-bounce. Even spacing, centred composition. Portrait orientation, aspect ratio 2:3. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark (shirt numbers are allowed only when the scene mentions them).

A character design sheet for an illustrated children's adventure novel about football.

Reference images attached, in this order: (1) an earlier watercolour character sheet of the two brothers (BriBri on the left, Kaydoudou on the right): copy their identity (faces, hair, skin tone, build, relative height, outfits) but NOT its watercolour style.

1. Subject: BriBri (age 12), the elder brother: slim and tall for his age with long limbs and narrow shoulders, dark brown skin with a warm undertone, a long oval face with high cheekbones, a slim jaw and slightly prominent ears, very short tightly curled black hair with ONE THIN SHAVED PART LINE ON THE LEFT SIDE of his head, calm attentive almond-shaped dark brown eyes, a small one-sided smile; upright, still posture. Kaydoudou (age 10), the younger brother: a head shorter than BriBri (the top of his head reaches his brother's chin), compact and springy, dark brown skin with a warm undertone, a round face with full round cheeks and a small nose, a small soft round black afro, big round sparkling mischievous dark brown eyes, a wide grin with a visible GAP BETWEEN HIS TWO FRONT TEETH; always up on his toes. He always wears ONE RED SOCK ON HIS RIGHT FOOT AND ONE YELLOW SOCK ON HIS LEFT FOOT.

2. Outfit and details: BriBri wears his faded forest-green football shirt with white collar, white sleeve trim and a white number 8 on the chest, black shorts, white socks, worn black boots, and holds under his left arm an old brown leather ball with a hand-sewn square patch of red-and-yellow wax-print cloth; a thick notebook covered in green and orange wax cloth is tucked in his right hand. Kaydoudou wears a sun-yellow T-shirt with one vertical red stripe on the left side of his chest, green shorts with a thin white side stripe, ONE RED SOCK ON HIS RIGHT FOOT AND ONE YELLOW SOCK ON HIS LEFT FOOT, scuffed white trainers.

3. Action and emotion: They stand side by side, full body, three-quarter front view, close enough that Kaydoudou's shoulder touches his brother's arm. BriBri stands tall and still with a calm small side smile, looking slightly ahead; Kaydoudou is caught mid-bounce on his toes, fists up, laughing with the gap in his front teeth showing. The top of Kaydoudou's head reaches exactly BriBri's chin.

4. Location: A clean character-sheet backdrop: a warm cream-to-sand vertical gradient with a soft painted ground shadow, no scenery.

5. Lighting: Clear warm key light from the upper left, a cool turquoise rim light from the right, two-step cel shadows on faces and clothes.

6. Composition and camera: Full-body, eye level at the height of Kaydoudou's chest, both figures centred with generous margins, feet visible.

7. Style: Cinematic stylized 3D-animation look translated into a hand-painted illustration: deliberate cel-shaded shadow shapes with two or three clean value steps, sculpted dimensional forms, angular but appealing shape design, visible painterly brush texture on surfaces and backgrounds, expressive child faces with clear readable features, rich saturated colour held together by one dominant palette, controlled dramatic lighting with a clear key light and a coloured rim light, atmospheric depth with soft haze between planes, composed like animated-feature key art. Warm, joyful, adventurous, for readers aged 8 to 12. Recognisably childlike proportions. Avoid: clip-art look, flat shading without depth, plastic or glossy toy skin, airbrushed faces, photorealistic adult faces on children, neon glow, bloom, lens flare, muddy darkness, clutter, mixed media, chibi proportions.

8. Output: Portrait 2:3. No text, no labels, no logos.

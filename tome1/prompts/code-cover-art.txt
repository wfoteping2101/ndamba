Cover artwork (no lettering) for a football coaching book about an African philosophy of possession with purpose, in the same painted visual world as an illustrated children's saga.

1. Subject: Small distant figures only, no close-up faces.

2. Outfit and details: Eleven small players in plain dark green kits and eleven opponents in plain white kits, no numbers readable, no logos.

3. Action and emotion: Luminous golden lines of passes connect the green players in a purposeful pattern of triangles that travels forward from their own half into the opponent's penalty area, like a glowing constellation drawn on the grass; the ball glows at the tip of the last pass.

4. Location: A packed African stadium at dusk seen from high above one end, the crowd waving red, yellow and green flags without any emblem, floodlight towers, a city skyline with palm trees beyond the stands.

5. Lighting: Golden dusk turning to deep teal at the top of the sky, where one single bright five-pointed star shines; floodlights warming the pitch.

6. Composition and camera: High wide aerial view, the pitch on a slight diagonal in the lower two thirds, the star high in the centre.

7. Style: Cinematic stylized 3D-animation look translated into a hand-painted illustration: deliberate cel-shaded shadow shapes with two or three clean value steps, sculpted dimensional forms, angular but appealing shape design, visible painterly brush texture on surfaces and backgrounds, expressive child faces with clear readable features, rich saturated colour held together by one dominant palette, controlled dramatic lighting with a clear key light and a coloured rim light, atmospheric depth with soft haze between planes, composed like animated-feature key art. Warm, joyful, adventurous, for readers aged 8 to 12. Recognisably childlike proportions. Avoid: clip-art look, flat shading without depth, plastic or glossy toy skin, airbrushed faces, photorealistic adult faces on children, neon glow, bloom, lens flare, muddy darkness, clutter, mixed media, chibi proportions.

8. Output: Portrait 7:10, top 34 percent calm sky for a title, bottom 16 percent darker stands for a subtitle. No text, no letters, no logos, no flags of real countries.

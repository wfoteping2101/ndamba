# NDAMBA: Book Content Map (Lionceaux tome 1 and Le Code Njangi)

Research dossier for the NDAMBA 2D pixel-art football game (Godot 4). Every fact comes from `tome1/` (BIBLE.md, STYLE.md, OUTLINE.md, art/CHARACTERS.md, notes/part1.md, chapters 01 to 40) and `coach/` (DRILLS.md, OUTLINE.md, chapters 04, 05, 25, drills/*.json). Game proposals are marked [addition] and need William's approval. Companion data: `content/academy/drills_index.json`, built by `tools/build_drills_index.py`.

## A. Lionceaux tome 1: what the game can use

Saga facts (BIBLE.md): BriBri is 12 and Kaydoudou 10 in Part I; the family has no surname; motto « Le ballon doit voyager. Mais il doit savoir où il va. »; Part I gives the first Njangi piece, le cœur. Part I matches are 8 a side, two halves of twenty minutes (ch. 3); training games are 5v5 (ch. 4) and 4v4 (ch. 6). The 5v5 engine is therefore a game choice. Quotes below are verbatim; speech tags such as « dit BriBri » are dropped, the convention of the existing source map.

### Chapter 1, Le ballon rapiécé (POV BriBri)
Saturday morning, dry season (« dix jours sans pluie »), friendly on the red-earth pitch. Kaydoudou dribbles five Gars and loses the ball to the sixth; the Gars score. Ousmane blocks a shot and throws to Mireille, 2-2. Last action: rebound off Kaydoudou's shin, BriBri one touch to Radio, Radio one touch to Mireille, 3-2 (« Trois passes. Quatre secondes. »). After the whistle Kaydoudou's « frappe du tonnerre » lands Mami's ball on Monsieur Bilé's barbed wire: leather split, bladder punctured. Evening: Papa's 1990 video and the Roger Milla dance, Maman's rule, BriBri's secret sheet in his maths book, Toussaint holidays announced.
Place: Bonamoussadi pitch, the house. Present: BriBri, Kaydoudou, Mireille, Ousmane, Prosper « Radio », Tonton Fokou, Papa, Maman.
Football: Lionceaux 3-2 Gars de la Pharmacie (Mireille twice; Gars scorers unnamed). Lesson 1: a dribble past five tires the ball; three passes get it there faster.
Quotes: « Le ballon doit voyager ! » ; « Aujourd'hui, je dribble toute la ville ! » ; « Mon tibia est très intelligent. » ; « Pas de devoirs, pas de crampons. » ; « Mami répare tout ! »

### Chapter 2, Le njangi de Mami (POV Kaydoudou)
Bus to Bafoussam (car-sick Kaydoudou, prune sellers, fog like spilt milk). Mami: « Il n'est pas mort. Il est blessé. » The vulcanisateur at the crossroads patches the bladder for free; Mami sews a square of red and yellow wax (her 1998 njangi dress) on her forty-year-old pedal machine; the ball now has a bump and rolls crooked. Fog game on the cousins' hillside with a plastic-bag ball: Kaydoudou dribbles two boys and a goat, then kicks toward BriBri's voice; Ernest scores unseen. Sunday njangi: 22 women in blue and white pagne, black ledger, green metal box, Mama Kemadjou treasurer, the box goes to Maman Tchinda. Mami gives the ball its mission.
Place: the bus, Mami's courtyard and ocher room, the crossroads, the hillside field. Present: Mami, Papa, Maman, the brothers, cousins Joseph and Ernest, the njangi women.
Football: street game in fog, no score. Lesson 2: the ball is the njangi box; a pass that goes nowhere is worthless.
Quotes: « Il a une cicatrice, comme les gens qui ont vécu. » ; « Ce qui ne circule pas pourrit, mon fils. » ; « Maintenant, ce ballon a une mission. Il doit voyager. » ; « Et dans le brouillard, on passe à la voix. »

### Chapter 3, Les Requins de Bépanda (POV BriBri)
Fokou announces the tournoi des quartiers: eight teams, two pools of four, pool games in November and December, semi-finals in January drawn from a cap, then the final. Bulldozer mocks the Lionceaux (« une équipe de chiffons ») and the ball (« Il a les oreillons »). Line-up: Ousmane goal, BriBri and Radio midfield, Kaydoudou left, Mireille right, Samuel up front. Mireille 1-0 at the 12th minute from BriBri's pass after an Ousmane save; Bulldozer puts two Requins on Kaydoudou, who keeps dribbling into the cage; Bulldozer equalises, shoulder-barges BriBri and sets up the 1-2. Row under the mango tree; Mireille scolds both; Fokou: « Vous avez gardé la caisse. » First poing contre poing, main sur le cœur.
Place: Bonamoussadi at sunset. Present: the team, Fokou, Bulldozer and the Requins, Madame Ekotto, children on Bilé's wall.
Football: Requins 2-1 Lionceaux. Lesson 3: when two close you down the ball must leave at once; whoever sees the free man must say it.
Quotes: « On dirait une équipe de chiffons. » ; « Tu ne passes jamais. » ; « Tu fais toujours le chef ! » ; « Le ballon doit voyager. Les mots aussi. » ; « Poing contre poing. » ; « Main sur le cœur. »

### Chapter 4, La boue et le pagne (POV Kaydoudou)
The rains turn the pitch into a red pond with islands and a lake in front of goal; the goal leans more. Training: bamboo sticks in triangles, dribbling forbidden (laps of the mango tree), 3v1 passes along the ground. Hidden behind Ousmane, Kaydoudou « n'existe pas », sulks, copies slow Jules: two steps and the lane reopens. 5v5 without dribbling, mud fight, Fokou's cap hit. Next Saturday vs Aigles de Makepe on a heavy pitch: the ten-pass ritual with Mami's ball is born; 0-0 at half; three minutes from time Kaydoudou takes two steps, receives from BriBri and squares to Samuel.
Place: Bonamoussadi in rain. Present: the team, Jules, Fokou, Madame Ekotto.
Football: training; Lionceaux 1-0 Aigles de Makepe (Samuel, assist Kaydoudou). Lesson 4: offer a solution; behind an opponent you do not exist; two steps and you are visible.
Quotes: « Montre-toi ! » ; « Tu n'existes pas. » ; « Je suis visible ! » ; « Tu bouges trop. » ; « J'ai passé ! Vous avez vu ? J'ai passé ! »

### Chapter 5, La passe polie et la passe utile (POV BriBri)
Last pool match vs Cobras de Bonabéri, grey, 0-0; BriBri passes sideways to applause (« Propre ! »), sees Radio alone in the gap, fears failure, plays safe. Mireille's free kick 1-0 in the last minute, Cobras equalise at once. Standings: Requins first with three wins, Lionceaux second, one goal ahead of the Cobras; the draw gives the Requins again. Fokou's mango: 34 passes, two or three toward goal. Papa draws packets on a network: a packet that loops never arrives. Night sketch "polie / utile", pencil breaks; BriBri decides to dare.
Place: pitch, Madame Ekotto's corner, living room. Present: the team, Fokou, Madame Ekotto, Monsieur Bilé, Papa.
Football: Lionceaux 1-1 Cobras (Mireille). Lesson 5: a sideways pass keeps the ball (polite), a pass through a line brings the goal closer (useful); do not fear the second.
Quotes: « Propre ! Très propre, BriBri ! » ; « C'est un comptable, ce petit. » ; « Une passe sur le côté, c'est poli. » ; « Le football n'est pas un salon, mon fils. » ; « Tu as le droit de rater une passe. »

### Chapter 6, La lettre de Waldorf (POV Kaydoudou)
Ndolé dinner; Papa's letter: Waldorf, Maryland, departure in January after the final. Kaydoudou runs to the night pitch and hides in the mango roots; Maman finds him (« Tu chantes quand tu pleures »). Night under mosquito nets: BriBri fears the plane (« deux cents tonnes »), Kaydoudou fears being forgotten; pinky promise never to play against each other. Mami on the phone: « L'eau qui coule ne perd pas sa source. » Saturday 4v4 on a dry patch: Kaydoudou sulks into the corner dragging a tall Makepe guest, BriBri passes into the hole, Mireille scores. Ousmane: « Alors on gagne le tournoi. »
Place: house, night pitch, pitch by day. Present: the family, Fokou, Radio, Mireille, Ousmane.
Football: 4v4 training, Mireille's goal, no score. Lesson 6: a player who moves stays in the team; a run without the ball drags a defender and opens space.
Quotes: « C'est quoi, Waldorf ? Une maladie ? » ; « On ne quitte pas sa maison, Kaydoudou. On l'emporte. » ; « Jamais jamais. » ; « Tu es très utile quand tu boudes. » ; « Un joueur qui bouge reste dans l'équipe. »

### Chapter 7, Le carnet de Tonton Fokou (POV BriBri)
Saturday 3 January, semi-final; Radio's father brings a real microphone; crowd on Bilé's wall, roofs and in the mango tree; casserole band. Ten passes, « Ni la caisse ni la bouche. » BriBri plays polite; 15th-minute corner, Bulldozer header through Ousmane's hands, 0-1. Half-time circle: BriBri stands and draws with a stick: play left to draw three Requins onto Kaydoudou, the hole in their line, Mireille forgotten on the right, Radio runs and talks, the two-finger signal. Fokou: « Vous avez entendu votre capitaine. » Fourth time the table tips, BriBri's pass through two blue shirts, Kaydoudou draws two and serves Mireille, 1-1; two minutes from time the une-deux du Njangi, Kaydoudou rounds the keeper, 2-1, Milla dance. Bulldozer's handshake. Fokou gives the notebook in green and orange calebasse pagne; BriBri recopies six lessons; final next Saturday, four tickets Douala, Paris, Washington, departure the Monday after.
Place: Bonamoussadi, full. Present: the team, Fokou, Bulldozer, Madame Ekotto, Radio's father, the parents.
Football: Lionceaux 2-1 Requins (Bulldozer; Mireille, Kaydoudou). Lesson 7: dare the pass that crosses; attract one side, play through the hole.
Quotes: « Ni la caisse ni la bouche. » ; « Il parle ! Mesdames et messieurs, il parle ! » ; « Vous avez entendu votre capitaine. » ; « Le une-deux du Njangi ! » ; « Pas mal, les chatons. » ; « Écris ce que le ballon t'apprend. »

### Chapter 8, Finale à Bonamoussadi (POV Kaydoudou)
Drums from six in the morning; Mami arrives by yellow taxi with ndolé, beignets and a black felt pen. Four o'clock, the Panthères de Deïdo, the tallest team, keeper « Monsieur Pieuvre ». Ten passes counted by the crowd. A wall against a wall: Kaydoudou bounces off a defender, then makes them move (quick release, two steps, decoy runs, a feint to draw two and a touch to Radio); BriBri directs with his hands; Ousmane dives three times; 0-0 at half (« on joue par terre »), 0-0 at the end. Shoot-out: Mireille, Samuel, Radio (off the shin), BriBri score; the fourth Panthère hits the bamboo post; Kaydoudou's « frappe du tonnerre » flies into Bilé's fence; Ousmane tips the captain's kick onto the post. Champions. Mami dances, Fokou cries (« la poussière »), three signatures on the ball. Monday at the airport: bracelet for Ousmane, Mireille's voice-message promise; take-off, hands held.
Place: Bonamoussadi, Douala airport, the plane. Present: the whole Part I cast, Mami, the Panthères captain and keeper.
Football: Lionceaux 0-0 Panthères, 4-3 on penalties (Mireille, Samuel, Radio, BriBri; Kaydoudou missed). Lesson 8: the heart of the Njangi: play together, make the ball travel, show yourself, dare and speak, nobody keeps the box.
Quotes: « Simple, c'est pour les comptables. » ; « ARRÊTÉ PAR LE BAMBOU ! » ; « Toi, tu rates. Moi, j'arrête. » ; « Cette poussière rouge, elle entre partout. » ; « Mes lions. » ; « Le ballon voyage. »

## C. Places and stadiums

Bonamoussadi red-earth pitch (Douala): red earth that becomes powder in the dry season and sticks to ankles, knees and eyelashes; two bamboo goals, one leaning more every chapter, a makeshift net that goes « chhh », a bamboo stub as corner flag; a mango tree in one corner where Fokou sits on his yellow bidon and where the half-time circle happens; benskins honking « Pin-pin ! » behind the mango tree and slowing on the road to read the score; Monsieur Bilé's plot with a barbed-wire fence and a low wall where children sit and chant « Kay-dou-dou ! »; Madame Ekotto's beignet table under a red parasol at the corner (« beignets gratuits pour celui qui marque »); tin roofs all round; a hundred steps from the house; Bépanda lies « de l'autre côté du canal ». Rain makes a red pond with islands and a lake in front of goal; the ball sticks « comme un escargot qui change d'avis ». Big matches: drums at dawn, a casserole band, Radio's father's microphone and loudspeaker, people on roofs and in the tree, Mami in the front row. The match ends when Fokou is hungry.

Bafoussam: courtyard of beaten earth with hens, ocher room with a glassless window, black pedal machine with faded gold flowers and an iron-grid pedal, the iron trunk of scraps, green hills, morning fog; the vulcanisateur at the crossroads; the cousins' hillside between maize fields, one goal uphill and one downhill, plastic-bag ball, goats.

Bépanda ground: not in the book; every Part I match is at Bonamoussadi. Deïdo: not in the book beyond the team.

Foreign grounds: Waldorf basement and driveway hoop, the futsal hall (9); the white dome on synthetic turf with black granules (10); grass pitches with spring mud (11); the twenty-pitch summer complex with tents and coolers (15); the Philadelphia stadium (14); ESM synthetic beside the Marne with a barge (17); a suburban away ground (20); a Brittany seaside pitch with granite houses and a lighthouse (23); La Granja del Mar between vines, pines and sea (25); Vinyamar square with plane trees, fountain and square bell tower (26); the beach with fishing boats and flip-flop goals (29); a small seaside stadium (32); Kingsbridge synthetic under floodlights in fine rain (33); a north London ground in snow (38); a real London stadium (40).

## F. Le Code Njangi: the 64 drills as academy missions

Framework (coach ch. 04, 05, 25). Four moments: possession; defensive transition (after the loss: Reprendre, five seconds of counter-press behind a rest defence); organised defence (block, triggers); offensive transition (after the recovery: Regarder devant, first look to depth). Five intentions of possession: Sortir (build out by drawing the opponent to free a man), Progresser (cross lines by pass, carry or run), Fixer (pin opponents to create a numerical, positional or qualitative superiority elsewhere), Finir (enter the finishing zone, shoot well), Respirer (keep the ball to reorganise, rest, manage score or heat); the drill files also tag the two reflexes Reprendre and Regarder devant as intentions. The single question: « Que prépare ta passe ? » Field words: Fixe, Respire, Sors, Reprends, Bloc, Devant. Ten skills with a 1 to 4 grid (discovery, construction, acquired, reference): scan, contrôle orienté, passe avec intention, conduite, dribble njangi, appels et courses pour les autres, communication, protection du ballon, contre-pression, prise de décision. Five pieces: le cœur, la raison, les yeux, la carte, le souffle. Families: C technique 14, R rondos 8, P position 10, S small games 8, L large games 6, F phases 6, T transitions 6, G finishing 6.

Tier rule (as coded in `drills_index.json`): Tier 1 = minimum players 8 or fewer, grid area 1200 m² or less, no coach signal (fits the 5v5 engine on a small grid); Tier 3 = 15 or more players, full or three-quarter pitch, or a core rule driven by a live coach signal (C06, R05, P08, T04; promotable if the HUD shows the colour or call); Tier 2 = everything else. Counts: Tier 1 = 24, Tier 2 = 24, Tier 3 = 16.

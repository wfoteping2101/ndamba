# Facts for the Ndamba guide (checked October 29, 2026)

Use only these facts for football history and the calendar. Do not add other real-world numbers. Do not name real players except Roger Milla (house rule of the whole project).

## Cameroon (the Indomitable Lions, federation FECAFOOT)
- 8 World Cup appearances, the African record: 1982, 1990, 1994, 1998, 2002, 2010, 2014, 2022. Source: Wikipedia, Cameroon national football team; Africanews, 14 Nov 2025.
- Best World Cup result: quarter-final in 1990 (Italy), the first African quarter-final; Roger Milla, 38 years old, scored four goals and danced at the corner flag.
- 5 Africa Cup of Nations titles: 1984, 1988, 2000, 2002, 2017.
- Olympic gold medal, Sydney 2000. FIFA Confederations Cup runner-up, 2003.
- Missed the 2026 World Cup: second in its qualifying group behind Cape Verde (Oct 2025), then beaten 1-0 by DR Congo in stoppage time in the African play-off semi-final in Rabat on 14 November 2025. Source: Africanews, 14 Nov 2025.

## Africa at the World Cup
- No African nation has ever won the World Cup.
- Best African result ever: Morocco, semi-final in 2022 (Qatar), fourth place.
- 2026 World Cup (USA, Canada, Mexico, first edition with 48 teams): Morocco was the last African team, beaten 2-0 by France in the quarter-final (Africanews, 10 Jul 2026). Spain won the final 1-0 after extra time against Argentina on 19 July 2026 at MetLife Stadium (worldcupranking.com).

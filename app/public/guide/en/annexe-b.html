Appendix B

French and English glossary

The words of Ndamba, of the method and of football, with their French equivalent as it appears in the app.

This glossary gathers the words you will meet in the app, in The Njangi Code and in this guide. The second column gives the word of the French edition: the one shown when the app is in French or, for words that are not on screen, the one used in the French edition of this guide. The last figure points to the chapter that explains it.

The platform and its pieces

Term In French Definition Chapter
Ndamba Ndamba Football, in Cameroonian street language. The name of the platform: the app, Mola, the books and the game. 1
Mola Mola "Mate", in camfranglais. Ndamba’s agent: it understands a sentence, prepares the work inside the app and waits for your consent before anything leaves it. 18
Pro club demo Démo club pro The demo of Étoile de la Côte, an invented professional club in Douala, coached by Daniel Mbarga. 5
U13 academy demo Démo académie U13 The demo of the Lionceaux de Bonamoussadi U13, the team of Kaydoudou and BriBri. 5
Role Rôle What you are in football: player, coach or educator, analyst, parent, manager or technical director, federation or league. It decides your home screen. 3
Plan Formule The offer you choose: Free, Player Premium, Coach Pro, Club and academy, Federation. Payments are not connected yet. 24
Backup Sauvegarde The file that holds all your data, exported from Settings and restored on another device. 6
The Njangi Code Le Code Njangi The method book for coaches, analysts and executives: the game model, the 64 drills and the Njangi Index. 19
Lionceaux Lionceaux The illustrated saga of Kaydoudou and BriBri. Book 1, Le Ballon qui voyage (The Travelling Ball), has 40 chapters. 20
Lionceaux Football Lionceaux Football Ndamba’s football game, played in the browser. 21

The method

Term In French Definition Chapter
Njangi Njangi The Cameroonian savings circle: everyone pays in their share, and the whole pot goes to each member in turn. In the method: the ball circulates for the whole team, with purpose. 2
Possession with purpose Posséder avec intention Keeping the ball to go somewhere, not for its own sake. The central idea of the method. 2
The seven intentions Les sept intentions What an action is trying to do: Build out, Progress, Pin, Finish, Breathe, Win it back, Look forward (in French: Sortir, Progresser, Fixer, Finir, Respirer, Reprendre, Regarder devant). 2
Game model Modèle de jeu The principles shared by every team of a club or a country, taught the same way at every age. 2, 22
The ten skills Les dix compétences The player’s grid, rated 1 to 4 by the staff: scanning, directional first touch, passing with purpose, carrying, Njangi dribble, runs for others, communication, ball protection, counter-pressing, decision making. 10
Ball Lesson Leçon du Ballon The lesson that ends each chapter of the novel. The 40 lessons can also be read in the player space. 16, 20

Training

Term In French Definition Chapter
Drill Exercice One of the 64 cards of the method, with its code (for example T01), its diagram, its coaching points and its variations. 7
Session Séance A dated sequence of drills, with its day in the match week (MD-4, MD-1…), its intention if you choose one, and its length. 8
Block Bloc A part of the session, with its minutes: most often a drill from the library, sometimes a free block you name yourself. 8
MD (match day) MD (jour de match) The marker of the week: MD is match day, MD-1 the day before, MD+1 the day after. 9
MD-4, tension MD-4, tension Four days before the match: small spaces, many accelerations and duels. The densest session. 9
MD-3, duration MD-3, durée Three days before: large spaces, play close to the match, for longer. 9
MD-2, speed MD-2, vitesse Two days before: short, fast drills, with plenty of rest between reps. 9
MD-1, activation MD-1, activation The day before: sharpness, set pieces, freshness. 9
MD+1, recovery MD+1, récupération The day after: active recovery for those who played, light rondos, stretching. 9
Load Charge A session’s planned effort: for each block, the minutes multiplied by the card’s intensity (low 1, medium 2, high 3, very high 4), then added up. 9

Team and match

Term In French Definition Chapter
Squad Effectif A team’s players, with their number, position and year of birth. 10
Player file Fiche joueur A player’s page: their skills, observations and journey. 10
Observation Observation A staff note about a player, most often taken during a session or a match. 10
Journey Parcours A player’s story season after season: sessions, matches, observations, evaluations, objectives. 10, 16
Lineup Composition The starters placed in the system, and the substitutes. 11
Game plan Plan de jeu What the team will do in each moment of the match: possession, defensive transition, defence, attacking transition, and set pieces. 11
Call-up Convocation The message to players and parents: the match, the meeting time, the list. Mola drafts it, you send it yourself. 11

Analysis

Term In French Definition Chapter
Live tagging Codage en direct Tapping a button for each action during the match. Each tap creates a tag. 12
Tag Tag A tagged action, with its moment in the match and, if you wish, its player. 12
Won back 5 s Reprise 5 s The ball won back within five seconds of losing it. 12
Njangi Index Indice Njangi The match score out of 100: did we possess with purpose? It is the sum of five sub-indices out of 20. 13
Progression Progression The sub-index of passes that move forward or break a line. 13
Penetration Pénétration The sub-index of entries into the final third and the box. 13
Fixation Fixation The sub-index of actions that draw two opponents and of the switches of play that exploit them. 13
Regain Reprise The sub-index of balls won back within five seconds of a loss. 13
Control Contrôle The sub-index of conservation: the share of actions where the ball stays with us (0 points at 55%, 20 points at 85% and above). 13
Conservation (K) Conservation (K) Completed passes divided by actions (completed passes, lost passes and losses). 13
Index bands Paliers de l’Indice Possession without purpose (under 35), Fragile purpose (35 to under 50), Njangi in progress (50 to under 65), Solid Njangi (65 to under 80), Championship Njangi (80 and over). 13
Match report Rapport de match The page that reads the tagged match: the Index, the sub-indices, the comparison, the drills to work on. 13
Video studio Studio vidéo The screen where you review a match video, place tags at the right moment and draw on the frame. The video stays on the device, and its tags do not count in the report. 14

The player, the family and Mola

Term In French Definition Chapter
Player space Espace joueur The player’s own space: play and lessons under 16, performance from 16. 15
Today’s challenge Défi du jour One of the three small challenges offered each day, to do alone, against a wall or with a few cones; it earns XP. 15
Njangi notebook Carnet du Njangi The notebook where the player writes what they have learned, like BriBri in the novel. 16
Task Tâche A piece of work Mola has done for you, with its steps. 18
To approve À valider The tab where everything that must leave the app (a message, a calendar) waits: nothing goes without your consent. 18

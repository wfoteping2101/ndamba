## For them

**To Kaydoudou and BriBri, my sons.**

You taught me that the ball travels better when we play together. This guide is the map of the road we are opening for you, and for every child who plays barefoot on red earth, dreaming of a star on the shirt.

**To Nevada, @nevadaney10, three years old,**

who was taken off a pitch because nobody could get the ball off him. May no child ever again be punished for their talent. Setbacks lead to greatness.

*William Foteping*

A match tells a story; a season tells a method. The Analysis page gathers every tagged match of a team and shows whether the Indice Njangi is rising, flat or falling, sub-index by sub-index. This is where a technical director sees whether the week's work shows up on Saturday. Video answers another question: how do you make players see what the numbers say? Many coaches already film with a phone, but a video file is heavy and mobile data is expensive. So Ndamba opens the video directly on the device, without ever sending it over the internet, and only saves what the coach adds to it: tags, notes and annotated images.

## The Analysis page: the season at a glance

In the menu, under **Analysis**, tap **Analysis**. On a phone, it is the **Analysis** tab in the bottom bar.

![The Analysis page of the pro club demo: on the left, the next match against Rapides de Kribi with the Go live button, two other upcoming matches and the list of analysed matches; on the right, the Indice match after match, the season sub-index by sub-index at 67.9 and the Video analysis card.](shot:pro-analysis-full "The Analysis page: what is left to tag, what has been tagged, and the season.")

### To tag live

At the top, **To tag live** lists the upcoming matches. The first one fills a dark card, **Next match**: date, competition, opponent, time, venue and the gold **Go live** button, with this reminder: "Two halves of 45 min. The phone stays awake while you tag." The following ones come as rows, each with its own button. A match already under way, or one that carries tags, shows **Resume live**; a planned match whose date has gone by carries a **Date passed** label. The **New match** link creates one (chapter 11).

On the free plan, one match can be tagged live. Beyond that, the card says "Unlimited live tagging is in Coach Pro" and the button becomes **See the plans**. Payments are not connected yet: activating a plan is a demonstration (chapter 24).

### Analysed matches

The **Analysed matches** list shows each tagged match, newest first: a small dial with the rounded Indice (68), the opponent, the date, the band (**Solid Njangi**), the number of tags, the score and the **Win**, **Draw** or **Defeat** label. Finished matches with no tags at all come next, with a grey trophy and **No tags**: in the demo, the matches against Éclair de Garoua (1-1) and Avenir du Mungo (2-0). Tap a row to open the report (chapter 13).

A finished match with no tags can still be tagged afterwards. Open its report and tap **Go live**: the live tagger accepts a finished match and says so: "Match finished: new tags complete the report."

### The Indice match after match

On the right, the card **The Indice match after match** draws the season's curve. With a single analysed match, as in the pro club demo, it shows that match's dial and band and warns: "The curve appears from the second analysed match." From two matches on, it draws a line from 0 to 100, with the 35, 50, 65 and 80 thresholds as guides, one point per match from oldest to newest, and the latest value. The line above says how many matches count: "2 matches analysed. Tap a point to open its report."

### The season, sub-index by sub-index

Under the curve, **The season, sub-index by sub-index** gives the season's Indice in large type (67.9 in the pro club demo, **Solid Njangi**) and the five bars. The calculation follows the method's rule, repeated under the title: "The codes are added up, then the Indice is computed: never the average of the matches." A match tagged with 700 tags therefore weighs more than a half-tagged one, which is fair: it holds more observed play. With a single match, the season's Indice is that match's Indice.

### The Indice in two sentences, and video

Below the matches, **The Indice Njangi, in two sentences** recalls the starting idea, "Possession percentage lies: a team can have the ball and never go anywhere.", then the motto and the link **Read The Njangi Code, chapter 32**. The **Video analysis** card lists the three latest projects and opens the studio with **Open video analysis**.

If no team exists yet, the page first asks you to create one, **Create your team first**: a **Team name**, an **Age group** (U9 to Seniors), a **Format** (5 v 5 to 11 v 11), then **Create the team**.

## Video: what stays on the device

The video studio reads a file that sits on the device: the phone, tablet or computer that filmed or received the video. The file is never sent over the internet. Ndamba saves in that device's browser the project title, the file name, its duration, your tags, your notes and your annotated images. It does not keep the video itself: each time you come back to the project, you reopen the same file, and your tags fall back into place at the right moment.

## Creating a video project

1. In the menu, under **Analysis**, tap **Video**. On a phone, tap **More**, then **Video**, or **Open video analysis** from the Analysis page.
2. The **Video analysis** page opens. With no project yet, it shows **No video project** and this advice: "Film with a phone and a tripod, then open the file here." At the bottom, **Analyse a video in three steps** sums up the method: **Film** (a phone on a tripod, up high, level with the halfway line, the whole pitch in frame), **Tag** and **Show** ("Five clips are enough for the video session.").
3. Tap **New project**.
4. In the **New video project** window, pick the **Linked match (optional)**, or leave **No match**. Picking a match fills in the **Title**, here "Étoile de la Côte v Flamme de Bertoua". You can write your own, for example "Lionceaux v Requins, second half".
5. Tap **Create the project**. The studio opens.

![The New video project window: the Linked match (optional) field set to Nov 8, v Flamme de Bertoua, the title filled in automatically, the line reminding you that the file is never sent over the internet, and the Cancel and Create the project buttons.](shot:pro-video-new "Creating a project: a linked match if you like, a title, and you are ready.")

The **Analyse the video** button of a match report does the same in one move: it opens the project linked to that match, or creates it.

## The video studio

At the top of the studio: the **Video projects** link back to the list, the **CSV** and **JSON** buttons (active from the first tag), the project title with a pencil, **Rename the project**, and the **Linked match (optional)**, which you can change at any time. When a match is linked, **Linked match report** opens its report.

### Opening the video

![The video studio before the file is opened: the dark Open a video area with the Choose the file button, the video's Indice Njangi at 0.0 with No tags yet, the empty Clips card and the greyed-out codes under the title Tag at 00:00.](shot:pro-video-studio-empty "Before the video: one button, and every code is waiting.")

The large dark area reads **Open a video**: "Choose a video file on this device (MP4 or WebM). It is not uploaded anywhere." Tap **Choose the file**, or on a computer drag the file onto the area. Until the video is open, the codes stay greyed out and the studio says "Open the video first." A file that is not a video is refused: "This file is not a video."

When you come back, the area reads "Choose the video "match-demo.mp4" again", with the explanation: "To save memory, Ndamba does not keep the video. Reopen the same file to find your tags at the right moment." If the file you pick has a different name, a banner warns that the tags will be placed on this video. The **Change video** link opens another one.

### Playing, slowing down, stepping frame by frame

![The studio with a demo video open, an animated tactics board with two teams of eleven dots, the timeline under the frame, the play, 5 second jump and frame step buttons, the time 00:00 of 00:24, the 0.5x, 1x and 2x speeds and the pencil for drawing.](shot:pro-video-studio "The studio with a demo video: an animated tactics board.")

Under the frame, from left to right:

- **Play** and **Pause** (tapping the frame also plays or pauses);
- **Back 5 s** and **Forward 5 s**;
- **Previous frame** and **Next frame**, to move one frame at a time (1/25 of a second);
- the time, for example "00:00 / 00:24";
- the speeds **0.5x**, **1x** and **2x**;
- the **Draw** pencil.

Above the buttons, the video timeline carries a coloured mark for each tag and the gold playhead. Tap the timeline to jump to a moment, or a mark to go to that tag. On a computer, the keyboard is enough: Space, the arrows (5 seconds), comma and full stop (one frame), D to draw, N for a note, and the live tagging keys for the codes (1 to 9, F, S, L, K, R, A, B, plus Q, W and E for the opponent). On a phone, the player stays pinned to the top while you scroll down to the codes.

### Tagging at the right moment

The code card takes the video time as its title, **Tag at 00:00**, and reminds you: "The tag lands at the moment shown by the video." These are the live tagging codes (chapter 12), in five groups: **Possession** (**Pass**, **Pass lost**, **Progressive**, **Line broken**), **Danger** (**Final third**, **Box**, **Shot**, **On target**, **Goal**), **Fixation** (**Pins two**, **Switch**), **Transitions** (**Loss**, **Won back 5 s**, **Ball won**) and **Opponent** (**Their shot**, **Their goal**, **Their pass**, **Their lost pass**, **Their final third**). Each button shows how many times it has been used, for example "×3", and a message confirms each tag ("Pass, 00:00").

The studio applies the same rules as live tagging: a **Goal** adds **Shot** and **On target**, an **On target** adds **Shot**, and a **Ball won** tapped within 5 seconds of a **Loss** or a **Pass lost** becomes **Won back 5 s**. Under the codes, the **Note** field takes a free sentence ("the pivot turns") with **Add the note**.

The good method: pause, step back frame by frame to the exact moment, tap the code, play again. The **0.5x** speed helps with fast phases.

### The video's Indice Njangi

On the right, the card **The video's Indice Njangi**, "Computed from this video's tags.", gives the score, its band and the five sub-indices as small bars. It updates with every tag.

![The studio after three Pass tags at 00:00: the video's Indice Njangi at 20.0 in the Possession without purpose band, Control at 20 out of 20 and the four other sub-indices at 0, the list of three clips and the Pass, 00:00 message.](shot:pro-video-studio-tagged "Three passes tagged: Control is perfect, and the Indice does not mean anything yet.")

The screenshot shows a trap worth understanding. Three completed passes and no loss give a Control of 20 out of 20, and nothing else: the Indice is 20.0, **Possession without purpose**. The Indice only makes sense over many actions. The match report warns below 12 tags; the video card does not.

> [!WARNING] Video tags stay in the video
> Today, the tags of a video project feed the video's Indice, its clips and its exports. They are not added to the match report or to the season, even when the project is linked to the match. For a match to count in the report and the season curve, tag it with the live tagger, which also accepts a finished match.

### Clips

The **Clips** card lists every tag in video order, with the total count: the time, the code's coloured icon, its name, the codes added automatically ("+ Shot, On target"), the note if there is one, the thumbnail of the annotated image, and the **Delete this tag** bin, which also removes the codes added with it. Tap a clip: the video restarts 4 seconds before the moment, so you see the move being built. As soon as two groups are present, chips let you filter: **All**, **Possession**, **Danger**, **Fixation**, **Transitions**, **Opponent** or **Note**.

A clip is a marked moment in the video, not a cut-out file. For the video session with the players, you replay the clips from this list, on the same device.

### Drawing on the frame

1. Tap the **Draw** pencil (or the D key). The video pauses and the word "Drawing" appears on the frame.
2. Pick the tool, **Arrow**, **Circle** or **Freehand**, and the colour, **Gold**, **White** or **Red**. Circles are slightly flattened, like players seen from the stand.
3. Draw with a finger or the mouse. **Undo stroke**, **Redo the stroke** and **Clear** fix mistakes.
4. Tap **Save the image**. The frame and the drawing are merged into a small image, attached to the selected tag if it is very close to this moment, or otherwise to a new note at this moment. The message confirms: "Image saved on the tag at 00:12".
5. **Close drawing** (or Escape) closes the tools without saving.

The thumbnail appears in the clip. Tap it to enlarge it: **Download the image** saves it on the device as a JPEG, **Remove the image** detaches it from the tag, **Go to the clip** replays the moment.

### Exporting: CSV and JSON

The two buttons at the top of the studio download the work to the device, without sending anything:

- **CSV**: one line per tag (columns time_s, time, code, label, side, note, drawing and group), to open in a spreadsheet, for example "ndamba-etoile-de-la-cote-v-flamme-de-bertoua.csv".
- **JSON**: the full project, the video's Indice and every tag, images included. This is the format to keep or to hand to an analyst.

## The list of projects

![The Video analysis page with one project: Étoile de la Côte v Flamme de Bertoua, a dial at 20, the linked match of Nov 8, the file match-demo.mp4, a duration of 00:24, 3 tags and the Possession without purpose band, then the three steps Film, Tag and Show.](shot:pro-videos-list "The list of projects: each card sums up one video and its Indice.")

On **Video analysis**, each project has its card: the dial of its Indice, the title, the linked match or the creation date, the file (or **No video yet**), the duration, the tags, the images and the band. The whole card opens the studio. The **Delete the project** bin asks for confirmation, "Delete this project and its tags?", before erasing. The video itself stays untouched on the device.

> [!INVESTOR] A video that costs nothing to carry
> Never uploading the video means zero storage cost for Ndamba, no mobile data spent by the coach and no image of a child hosted anywhere but on their own device. The studio therefore works wherever the device's browser can play the video. The trade-off is deliberate: no sharing of the video between staff members for now.

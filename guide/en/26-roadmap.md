What this inventory means for an investor fits in one word: coherence. The pieces are not just placed side by side, they are connected. The 64 drills come from the book and feed the sessions. The match report computes the Index with the book's formulas and recommends drills for the weakest sub-indices. Mola uses the same functions as the screens. The novel's lessons feed the youngest players' space, and the game uses the novel's clubs. The whole thing speaks one vocabulary, the seven intentions, in two languages. Rebuilding it would mean writing a method, not just code.

## What comes next

The work below is planned. None of it has an announced delivery date; where a year appears, it is the year of the plan stage that needs it.

| Work | Why | Depends on | Plan stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts and sync | Share between staff, players and parents; measure usage with everyone's consent | The principles of chapter 25, hosting | Live during stage 2 (2028 to 2029) |
| Payments | Collect plan revenue; the Premium page announces Mobile Money and cards as "coming soon" | A payment provider | During stage 2 at the latest |
| Staff accounts | Several staff members on the same team | Accounts | Stage 2 |
| Plan limits | Align the code with the Premium page (only two limits exist today) | Payments | With payments |
| Shared video | Circulate clips and drawings within a club | Accounts, storage | After accounts |
| Coach education | 100 coaches trained, then the first certified Njangi coaches | Trainers, modules | Stages 1 and 2 |
| Index references by age | Know what a good Index looks like at U13 or U17 | 1,000 tagged matches, shared with consent | Stages 1 and 2 |
| National game model and national teams view | Get U15, U17 and U20 working on one model | A partnership with a federation | Stage 3 (2030) |
| *Lionceaux*, book 2: *La Tanière des Lionceaux* | Continue the saga, then books 3 to 6 | Writing and illustration | No announced date |
| More languages | Reach more players, families and federations | Translating the app and the books | At the pace of the federations that join the model |

The titles of the later books are already set: *Premier contrat*, *La CAN*, *Mondial 2030* and *2034, l'étoile*.

**In what order, and why.** A payment has to be tied to a person, so payments and accounts move forward together, and they come first, because they determine revenue, sharing between staff and families, and the measurement of usage all at once. Coach education and pilot clubs, on the other hand, do not wait for code: they can start today with the app as it is. Age references need data: during stage 1 it comes from backups shared with consent, then from accounts. Shared video and staff accounts naturally follow accounts. The national teams view and the national game model come when a federation commits. The books and the languages move in parallel, with other trades: writing, illustration, translation.

## The indicators to follow

An indicator is only useful if it can be measured. The app contains no audience measurement tool and reports nothing by itself: during stage 1, measurement therefore relies on what pilot clubs declare and on the backups they agree to share.

| Indicator | Proposed target | How to measure it today |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot clubs and academies active every week | 20 (stage 1) | Club declarations, weekly backups shared with consent |
| Coaches trained in the method | 100 (stage 1) | A training register |
| Tagged matches | 1,000 (stage 1) | Shared backups: each tagged match is in them with its tags |
| Index references by age group | One per age group (stage 2) | Calculated from the tagged matches |
| Teams on the shared model | 300 (stage 2) | Not measurable without accounts |
| Certified Njangi coaches | The first ones (stage 2) | A certification register, to be created |
| Active paid plans | No numeric target | Not measurable: payments are not connected |
| National teams on the national model | U15, U17 and U20 (stage 3) | Agreement with the federation |
| Senior team results | A quarter-final or better in 2034, the trophy in 2038 | The World Cup record |

With accounts, the first four lines can be counted without effort, provided everyone agrees.

Numbers are not enough. Three qualitative signals matter just as much, and you collect them by talking to the pilot coaches: does **Prepare the week** save them time? Do they read the report after each match, and change their sessions as a result? Do players and parents come back to the challenges and the lessons on their own? A download count, on the other hand, says nothing about any of this and is not tracked.

## What the roadmap does not include

Some options would be easy and would pay quickly. They are not in the plan, because they would damage trust or the method:

- advertising in the app, let alone in front of children;
- a ranking of young players, which the app already rules out up to the age of 16;
- paid books or paid drills;
- real players used as a showcase, or results announced without the tagged matches that prove them.

## A typical pilot, proposed

So that stages 1 and 2 are measured the same way everywhere, here is the proposed sequence for a pilot club. It is a proposed way of working, not a programme already under way.

1. **Train.** The coach reads the parts of *The Njangi Code* on intentions, the week and measurement, and gets a short training in tagging.
2. **Set up.** With Mola, the coach creates the space, the team and the squad in the welcome conversation.
3. **Work every week.** The coach prepares the week towards the match, runs the sessions, tags the match live and reads the report.
4. **Back up.** Every week the coach exports a backup and, if they agree in writing, shares it for the calculation of the references.
5. **Review every month.** The Index and its sub-indices are read together with the coach, and next month's intentions are chosen accordingly.

## How an investor or partner can help

Each kind of help matches a specific piece of work:

- **Fund stage 2**: accounts, sync and payments, plus an external review of how minors' data is protected.
- **Open doors**: introduce clubs and academies in Douala, Yaoundé, Bafoussam and the diaspora, to form the first circle.
- **Train**: bring coach educators who can teach the method and help build the certification.
- **Payments**: a Mobile Money partner or a payment provider.
- **Federations and leagues**: start a conversation; the plan is built on quote, with the national game model and coach education.
- **Content**: writing and illustration for book 2, translation into more languages.

The point of contact is the project's author, William Foteping, for Lemon Network Solutions, Ndamba's publisher. None of these partnerships exists today: they are places waiting to be taken.

> [!INVESTOR] The roadmap in three sentences
> What exists is complete for stage 1: the method, the drills, the coach's tools, the measurement and the agent. What is missing is identified and ordered: payments and accounts first, then coach education and age references, then the national teams view for stage 3. The first indicator to watch is the number of matches tagged by pilot clubs, because the rest of the plan rests on it.

## Check it yourself in ten minutes

All of the above can be checked on a computer or a phone, without an account:

1. On the site's home page, tap **Pro club demo**. If this browser already holds your own space, the "Start the demo?" window warns you that the demo replaces its data: export a backup first, then tap **Start the demo**.
2. On **Home**, look at today's session and the next match, then tap **Prepare the week**. In the demo, every day before the match already has its session: a message says so and offers **See the week**.
3. Open **Analysis**, then the report of the 2-1 win over Météore de Bafoussam: a Njangi Index of 67.9.
4. Open **Mola** and type a request (see the box below); watch the steps tick off one by one.
5. Open **Premium**, tap **Activate (demo)** and read the message: "No payment was taken."
6. Open **Settings** and tap **Export**: the downloaded file holds all the demo's data, and it is on your device, nowhere else.
7. Open **Football game** and play a match.

> [!MOLA] A request to test the agent
> In the pro club demo, type: "Analyse my last match". Mola finds the 2-1 win over Météore de Bafoussam, counts the tags, computes the Njangi Index (67.9), sums up what worked and what to fix, and suggests drills for the weak point.

In Africa, many coaches work alone, with no assistant and no analyst. In the evening there is still the week to plan, the call-up to write, the dates to put in the calendar: that work eats into the time meant for football. Mola is Ndamba's agent. You tell it what you want in your own words, in French or in English, and it does it inside the app, step by step, in front of you. One rule has no exception: nothing leaves the app without your approval. Mola prepares the message or the file, and you are the one who sends it.

## Where to find Mola

- In the menu, just below **Home**, tap **Mola**. On a phone, tap **More**, then **Mola**.
- On every page of the app, Mola's round button waits in the bottom right corner (see below). It does not appear on the Mola page itself, nor during live tagging, where the screen belongs entirely to the match.
- The welcome conversation, when you set up your space, is already Mola (chapter 5).

The page opens with Mola's avatar and this line: "Ndamba's agent. It prepares inside the app; you approve anything that leaves it." Three tabs follow: **Chat**, **Tasks** and **To approve**.

## The chat

![The Mola page of the pro club demo: the Chat tab with the welcome message addressed to Daniel, two ideas under the Mola noticed heading with the Yes, draft it and Yes, analyse it buttons, the Ask Mola field, and on the right the Try asking column and the Nothing goes out without you box.](shot:pro-mola-chat "The chat: Mola welcomes you, notices what is coming up and offers to take care of it.")

In the demo, Mola greets the coach: "Welcome to the demo, Daniel. You coach Étoile de la Côte: 22 players, a week of sessions, a match already analysed and a match coming up on Saturday." At the bottom, the **Ask Mola…** field takes your request: Enter sends, Shift and Enter starts a new line.

On a computer, the **Try asking** column offers eight sentences: tap one and it goes out as if you had typed it. On a phone, the same ideas appear as chips above the field. A player or a parent gets other ideas, about lessons, drills and books ("Open lesson 1", "Which drills to scan better?"). Under the column, the **Nothing goes out without you** box restates the rule.

When Mola works, you see the work being done. It announces what it is doing ("I'm analysing your match against Météore de Bafoussam."), then the steps appear and tick one by one, like a passing move. The result follows: a short text, the items created or suggested, and a button to open it, for example **Read the full report**, **See the week** or **Open the session**. The **See the 4 steps** link lets you reread what was done. Under the answer, new chips offer the natural next step. And now and then, a planned week or session ends with the motto: "The ball must travel. But it must know where it is going."

### When Mola asks a question

If a detail is missing, Mola asks a single question, with ready-made answers as chips. Tried in the demo:

1. You: "Set up a match".
2. Mola: "Who are you playing against?"
3. You: "Lions de Makepe".
4. Mola: "Which day, and at what time if you know it?"
5. You: "Sunday at 10am". Mola schedules the match for Sunday 1 November at 10:00.

If the answer does not help, it asks once more ("Sorry, I didn't catch that."), then shows what it can do. "No" cancels: "Fine, I've cancelled it. Tell me when you want to pick it up again." A new, clear request replaces the question. And if a team is needed first, Mola asks for its name, creates it, then goes back to your request.

Mola also offers the next step, which a simple "yes" accepts. After a match is set up, the **Yes, plan the week** chip appears; after an analysis, **Plan the week around “Pin”**. Tap it, or answer "yes" or "go".

## "Mola noticed"

Under the conversation, a green box, **Mola noticed**, offers at most two ideas drawn from your real data, each with its own button:

| What Mola notices | What it offers | The button |
|---|---|---|
| You have no team yet | "You don't have a team yet. Shall we create it together?" | **Yes, let's create it** |
| The next match is two to six days away and no session is planned from MD-4 to MD-1 | "Your match against … is … and the week is empty. Shall I plan the four sessions?" | **Yes, plan the week** |
| The next match is four days away or less and the call-up has not been prepared | "The match against Rapides de Kribi is coming up (Saturday). Shall I draft the call-up?" | **Yes, draft it** |
| The last match, played seven days ago or less, is tagged and not yet summarised | "Your match against Météore de Bafoussam (2-1) is tagged. Shall I summarise it?" | **Yes, analyse it** |

The button starts the action directly, with no sentence to retype. The **Hide this idea** cross sets it aside for the day.

## Tasks

Every action request becomes a task, tracked in the **Tasks** tab. Until you have asked for something, it shows "No tasks yet" and the **Talk to Mola** button.

![The Tasks tab of the Mola page, still empty in the demo: the heading No tasks yet, the line Every request to Mola becomes a task tracked here, and the Talk to Mola button.](shot:pro-mola-tasks "The Tasks tab before the first request: every request will leave a trace here.")

Filters sort the list: **All**, **Running**, **To approve**, **Done** and **Cancelled**. Each task has a name (**Match analysis**, **Week planned**, **Session created**, **Match set up**, **Player added**, **Team created**, **Call-up**, **Calendar export**, **Drills suggested**, **Leçon du Ballon** or **Book**), a status (**Running**, **To approve**, **Done**, **Cancelled** or **Failed**), your request in quotes with the day and time, the summary, the result button and the step details. A waiting task shows **Review**.

## To approve: "Nothing goes out without you"

With Mola, only two things can leave the app: a message, the match call-up, by WhatsApp, text message or e-mail; and a calendar file (.ics). For these two, Mola prepares everything, then stops on a last step: "Waiting for your approval before opening anything". The task moves to **To approve**, and a gold badge counts the waiting tasks on the tab and on Mola's button.

![The To approve tab of the Mola page, empty in the demo: the heading Nothing to approve, the explanation that a message or a file leaving the app waits here for your approval, and the Draft a call-up button.](shot:pro-mola-approvals "The To approve tab when nothing is waiting: one button to draft the call-up.")

Sending the call-up for the next match:

1. Ask "Send the call-up on WhatsApp", tap **Yes, draft it** under "Mola noticed", or **Draft a call-up** in the **To approve** tab.
2. Mola finds the match, takes the line-up and drafts the message: the opponent, the day, the time, the venue, the starters, the substitutes, and the meeting time, "Please arrive 45 minutes before kick-off with both kits, water and shin pads."
3. The **Waiting for your approval** card appears: "Nothing goes out without you. When you approve, I open WhatsApp with the message ready: you choose the group and press Send."
4. Under **Send with**, choose **WhatsApp**, **SMS**, **E-mail** or **Copy**. Add a **Number (optional)** if you like, or an **E-mail address (optional)** and a **Subject**.
5. Under **Message**, tap **Edit** to change the text, then **Done**. Your changes are kept.
6. Tap **Approve and open WhatsApp** (or **Approve and open SMS**, **Approve and open e-mail**, **Approve and copy**). Your own app opens with the message ready. Pick the group and press Send yourself.

Mola confirms: "It's open in WhatsApp. Pick the team group and press Send: I never send anything on your behalf." The task moves to **Done**. If you tap **Cancel**, nothing goes out: "Cancelled, nothing went out."

For the calendar, the card lists the week's **Events**, sessions and matches, and **Approve and download** saves the .ics file. Open it to add the week to your calendar.

## Everything Mola can do today

| You ask | Mola does it inside the app | What leaves, with your approval |
|---|---|---|
| Plan the week | Creates the MD-4 to MD-1 sessions before the next match (or the match you name), built around the intention you give or, failing that, the one from the last report. It overwrites no existing session and does not fill days already gone. | Nothing |
| Create a session | Builds a session with drills from the method, based on the day (MD-4 to MD+1, or Youth), the intention and the length. With no day given, it works it out from the next match. | Nothing |
| Set up a match | Creates the match, suggests an automatic line-up in the team's system and a captain. It refuses to create the same match twice. | Nothing |
| Add players | Creates the profile of one or several players, with number and position (midfield by default), and warns you if the number is already taken. | Nothing |
| Create a team | Creates the team with its age group, format and starting system, and makes it active. | Nothing |
| Draft the call-up | Writes the message for the next match with the line-up. | The message, by WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail or copy |
| Export the week | Gathers the week's sessions and matches. | An .ics calendar file |
| Analyse the last match | Computes the Indice, writes what works and what to fix, suggests two drills. You can name the opponent. | Nothing |
| Suggest drills | Picks the three most useful drills for an intention or a skill, suited to the team's age. | Nothing |
| Open a Leçon du Ballon | Opens one of the novel's 40 lessons, with the link to its chapter. | Nothing |
| Open a book | Opens *The Njangi Code* or the novel *Le Ballon qui voyage*, at the chapter you ask for. | Nothing |

Mola also answers "What can you do?", greetings and thanks. On the free plan, it stops at five saved sessions and suggests Coach Pro, with the reminder that "the books and the 64 drills stay open to everyone".

What Mola does not do: it never sends anything itself, it deletes nothing, it does not change an existing session, and it does not tag matches for you. It is not built to answer general football questions: when it does not recognise a request, it says so and shows what it can do.

> [!TIP] Give the day when it matters
> Mola never creates the same match twice (same opponent, same day), but it does not warn you if another match already exists that day: check the match list. Likewise, if the MD-3 day of the next match has already gone by, "Create an MD-3 session" dates the session today. Add the day you want, for example "tomorrow".

## How Mola understands

Mola understands in two layers.

The first is a rules engine that runs in the browser, on your device. It reads the sentence in French or in English, whatever the interface language, and replies in the interface language. It tolerates small typos and missing accents ("create a recovery sesion for tomorow" is understood). It recognises days ("Saturday", "tomorrow", "November 12", "11/12"), times ("4pm", "15:30"), week codes ("MD-3", "MD+1"), the words of the method ("counter-press" gives the **Win it back** intention, "build out" gives **Build out**), positions ("full back", "striker") and channels (WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail). Once the app has loaded, this engine needs no network, costs nothing and answers at once. An automated test file checks it: 124 checks, all passing on 29 October 2026.

The second layer is optional: an artificial intelligence model. When the server hosting Ndamba has an Anthropic API key, each new request (not a plain yes or no, nor an answer to one of Mola's questions) is also sent to the server function /api/mola, which asks a Claude model what the message means, within the same list of actions and details. What you need to know:

- The model does nothing itself. It interprets the sentence; the same code then does the work, with the same steps and the same approvals.
- Its answer is checked: only known actions and well-formed details are kept, then combined with the rules.
- The browser waits 3.5 seconds at most. With no answer, the rules reply alone; after two failures, Mola leaves the model aside for the rest of the visit.
- For a greeting, a request for help or a sentence it does not understand, the model may write one or two sentences, with the instruction never to claim it has done something.
- What goes to the server: the text of the message (600 characters at most), the language, today's date, the team name, its age group, your role, and the opponent and date of the next and last matches. Not the players, not the tags, not the sessions.
- The key stays on the server and never reaches the browser.

Without a key, only the rules engine answers. That was the case in the version tested for this guide: every answer quoted here comes from the rules.

## Ten sentences that work

All of them were tried in the pro club demo on Thursday 29 October 2026, two days before the match against Rapides de Kribi.

| Type | What Mola does |
|---|---|
| "Analyse my last match" | Summarises the match against Météore de Bafoussam: "Solid Njangi: 67.9 out of 100", what works (Control), what to fix (Fixation), the drills R02 and S04, and **Read the full report**. |
| "Plan my week before the match" | Prepares the missing sessions before the next match. In the demo: "Your week is already ready: 2 sessions before this match. I haven't overwritten anything." |
| "Create an MD-3 session to progress" | Creates the session "Duration: play like the match", three blocks, 62 minutes, with the common thread "Progress". |
| "Set up a match against the Eagles on Saturday at 4pm" | Schedules the match for Saturday 31 October at 16:00, picks the line-up, then offers to plan the week. |
| "Add Samuel Owona, number 9, striker" | Creates the profile, number 9, striker, and warns that number 9 is already taken in the squad. |
| "Send the call-up on WhatsApp" | Drafts the call-up for the match against Rapides de Kribi and puts it in **To approve**. |
| "Export the week to my calendar" | Prepares an .ics file with the week's sessions and matches, waiting for your approval. |
| "Which drills to win the ball back faster?" | Suggests T01 (The Five Seconds), F06 (Rest Defence) and T06 (Attack, Then Defend). |
| "Open lesson 7" | "Lesson 7: “Dare the pass that cuts through”.", with the link to chapter 7 of the novel. |
| "Open The Njangi Code" | Opens the book: "the method, the 64 drills and the Indice Njangi". |

And also: "What can you do?" gives the full list, and "an MD-2 session of 60 minutes on counter-pressing" creates a 60 minute speed session built around **Win it back**.

> [!INVESTOR] An agent that works without a network and without a bill
> Mola rests first on rules that run on the device: once the app has loaded, it understands with no network and no cost per message, which matters where connections are rare and expensive. The AI model is only an optional layer that improves understanding, never a condition for working, and it receives a bare minimum of context. Above all, the agent cannot send anything on its own: every message and every file that leaves waits for the coach's approval. That is the basis of trust for clubs, parents and federations (chapter 25).
